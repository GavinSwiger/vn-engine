Rules for every picture

  1. Style: simple bright anime style, with bold clean outlines, flat colors, one soft shadow tone and large expressive eyes. Describe it in words. Don't name another game: ChatGPT may refuse, or copy that game's characters.
  2. Keep details low. A few strong features per character: hair, one accessory, a simple outfit. Heavy detail made the image step fail.
  3. Every character is an adult. "Adult" and "early 20s" are in every prompt.
  4. Romance wording: write "romantic", "tender", "close", "blushing". Not "sensual", "seductive" or "intimate"; those get refused or come out stiff.
  5. Keep hands close to the body on sprite sheets. A raised fist or open arms crosses into the next panel.
  6. Make Seraphine first. Once you like her style, upload her sheet when you start each other character so the cast matches.

Part 1Sprites

Each character gets 8 emotions, made as two sheets of 4 in one ChatGPT chat.

Sheet A
1neutral2happy3sad4angry
Sheet B
1surprised2embarrassed3worried4laughing

Steps

  1. Start a new chat for the character.
  2. Paste her Sheet A prompt (below).
  3. For every character after Seraphine, upload Seraphine's Sheet A image and add this line at the very top of the prompt: 
    Match the art style of the attached image exactly, but draw a different character.
  4. Check the sheet: 4 panels, one figure in each, same outfit and size in all four, nothing crossing the white lines. Fix anything wrong (see Fixing problems).
  5. In the same chat, paste the Sheet B prompt.
  6. Download both sheets and cut them into sprites (see Part 3).

Part 2Romance CGs

Two full-screen scenes per character, drawn from the player's point of view. Romantic, not sexual: fully clothed, built on closeness, blushing and eye contact.

Steps

  1. Finish the character's sprites first.
  2. Start a new chat and upload her Sheet A image. Every CG prompt starts by asking ChatGPT to match it.
  3. Paste one CG prompt. One CG per message.
  4. Check that her face is away from the top and bottom edges. ChatGPT usually makes wide images in 3:2, and the game crops them to its 16:9 screen, cutting a strip off the top and bottom. If her face is too close to an edge, ask: "Make it a 16:9 image with the same content."

Part 3Putting pictures in the game

Sprites

  1. Remove the grey background with any background-removal tool (remove.bg, or the "remove background" tool in most image editors).
  2. Cut the sheet along the white lines into 4 pictures.
  3. Save each as a PNG in a folder named after the character, named after the emotion:
game/images/seraphine/neutral.png       →  show seraphine neutral
game/images/seraphine/happy.png         →  show seraphine happy
game/images/seraphine/sad.png           →  show seraphine sad
game/images/seraphine/angry.png         →  show seraphine angry
game/images/seraphine/surprised.png     →  show seraphine surprised
game/images/seraphine/embarrassed.png   →  show seraphine embarrassed
game/images/seraphine/worried.png       →  show seraphine worried
game/images/seraphine/laughing.png      →  show seraphine laughing

Use lowercase letters, digits and _ only in file and folder names, with no spaces or hyphens: meilin, not Mei-Lin.

Size: ChatGPT's sprites come out about 1000 pixels tall, too big for the game's 720-high screen. Tell the game once, in game/config.toml:

[stage]
art_height = 1080

Height: every sprite is drawn at the same scale, with Mei-Lin shorter and Seraphine and Kesh taller. Don't resize one character to fill the frame, or they'll all look the same height on screen.

CGs

Save each CG in game/images/cg/ with the file name shown above its prompt, for example game/images/cg/seraphine_balcony.png. CGs fill the screen at any size; no setting is needed.

Add each CG to the Extras gallery with a line at the top level of a script (outside any scene). It unlocks for the player the first time the story shows it:

lock gallery cg seraphine_balcony "Moonlit Balcony"

label seraphine_balcony_scene:
    scene cg seraphine_balcony
    seraphine "I've never shown anyone this view before."

Fixing problems

Reply in the same chat. Don't start over unless the chat keeps failing.

ProblemWhat to do
"Image generation failed"Click Try again once or twice. Still failing: start a new chat and paste again. Still failing: cut details from the character line. If a short prompt fails too, ChatGPT is busy or you've hit your daily image limit. Wait a few hours.
One panel's outfit or hair is differentPanel 3 has a different outfit than the others. Redo it to match panels 1, 2 and 4 exactly.
One panel's expression is wrongRedo panel 2 only, keep everything else exactly the same.
Figures cross the white linesKeep each figure fully inside its own panel with empty space around her. Nothing may cross the white lines. If one gesture keeps crossing, change it to one closer to the body.
The image is square, not wideMake it a wide landscape image with the same content.
The style doesn't match the othersStart a new chat, upload Seraphine's Sheet A, and add the "Match the art style…" line at the top.
A refusal on a CGLook for words like "sensual" or "seductive" and swap them for "romantic" or "tender".
