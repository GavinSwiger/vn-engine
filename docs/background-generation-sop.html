Rules for every background

  1. Match the characters. Upload one of the character expression sheets in every new chat. The prompts tell ChatGPT to match its style.
  2. No people. The girls stand in front of the background. A crowd painted into it fights with them. Every prompt says "no people".
  3. No writing. Generators make fake letters on signs and books. Every prompt says "no text"; blank signs are fine.
  4. Eye level. The camera is a person standing in the place, not looking down from above. Sprites only look right at eye level.
  5. Open middle, soft detail. Keep the center and lower half uncluttered. That's where the girls stand and where the text box sits.
  6. Day first, then variants. Make the day version, then ask for evening and night in the same chat, so the layout stays the same.

Leaving room for characters

Put the interesting things (buildings, trees, the fountain, bookshelves) along the top and the sides. Leave the middle and the ground plain: a path, a floor, a field. The girls stand there, and the text box covers the bottom strip.

The game fills its 16:9 screen with each background. ChatGPT often makes 3:2 images, which are a bit taller, so a strip comes off the top and bottom. Don't put anything important right at those edges.

StepsHow to make one

  1. Start a new chat for each place.
  2. Upload one character sheet (any girl's Sheet A) for the style.
  3. Copy that place's prompt from The 12 places and send it. You get the day version.
  4. Check it: no people, no writing, eye level, open middle. Fix anything wrong (see Fixing problems).
  5. In the same chat, send the evening prompt, then the night prompt. Use the indoor versions for indoor places.
  6. Download all three and save them with the names shown (see Putting them in the game).

Send these in the same chat, right after the day version. They keep the layout and change only the light, which turns 12 places into 36 backgrounds.

Make this same image again with exactly the same layout, buildings, objects and camera angle. Only change the time of day to sunset: a warm orange and pink sky, long soft shadows, golden light on everything. Still no people and no text.

Night (outdoors)

Make this same image again with exactly the same layout, buildings, objects and camera angle. Only change the time of day to night: a deep blue sky with stars and a moon, lanterns and windows glowing with warm light, cool blue shadows. Still no people and no text.

Evening (indoors)

Make this same image again with exactly the same room, furniture, objects and camera angle. Only change the light to evening: warm golden sunset light coming through the windows, longer shadows, a cozier mood. Still no people and no text.

Night (indoors)

Make this same image again with exactly the same room, furniture, objects and camera angle. Only change the light to night: dark windows, the room lit only by candles, lanterns and any fire, warm pools of light and deep shadows. Still no people and no text.

Optional extra: a rainy version for sad or tense scenes. "Make this same image again with exactly the same layout. Only change the weather to a grey rainy day: falling rain, wet shiny ground with puddles and reflections." Save it as _rain.

Putting them in the game

  1. Open your game's background folder. In Studio, right-click Images ▸ bg in the Project panel and choose Open in File Explorer. For your test game it's C:\Users\Wispl\Desktop\vn-full-test\images\bg.
  2. Save each picture there as a PNG, named place_time, with lowercase letters and _ only:
images/bg/town_square_day.png       →  scene bg town_square_day
images/bg/town_square_evening.png   →  scene bg town_square_evening
images/bg/town_square_night.png     →  scene bg town_square_night
images/bg/slum_alley_rain.png       →  scene bg slum_alley_rain

Backgrounds fill the screen at any size, so they need no resizing (unlike the sprites).

Use one in a scene. with dissolve fades from the last place; with fade goes through black, good for a new day:

label market_morning:
    scene bg bazaar_day with fade
    show amara happy at left with dissolve
    amara "You're late. The best saffron sells before the bells."

Fixing problems

Reply in the same chat. Start over only if the chat keeps failing.

ProblemWhat to send
People in the pictureRemove every person from this image. Keep everything else exactly the same.
Writing on signs or booksRemove all letters and writing. Leave the signs blank. Keep everything else the same.
Something big in the middleMove the [fountain] to the side and leave the middle of the ground open. Keep the same style and light.
Looking down from aboveRedo it from eye level, as if a person is standing in the scene.
Evening or night changed the layoutKeep the exact layout of the day version. Only change the lighting. If it still drifts, upload the day image again with that sentence.
The style doesn't match the girlsStart a new chat, upload a character sheet, and use the prompt as it is. Its first sentence asks for the matching style.
The image is squareMake it a wide 16:9 landscape image with the same content.
"Image generation failed"Click Try again. Still failing: a new chat. Still failing: ChatGPT is busy or you've hit your daily image limit, so wait a few hours.
